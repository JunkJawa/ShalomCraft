# ShalomCraft changelog

Bump `mod_version` in `gradle.properties` for every release you hand to other people.
Install the same version on the server and on every client.

## 0.4.1
- Kippah and tallit now fit Accessories slots: kippah in the Hat slot, tallit in the Cape slot. They still work as armor if you prefer. Note: the worn look in the Accessories slots uses the mod's default item rendering, so tell us how it looks and we will tune it
- Visual polish: kippah and tallit icons redrawn with shading, gold rim and neckband, tassels. Worn textures gained cloth pleats, a gold neckband, a wide and thin stripe pair, and a gold crown button on the kippah

## 0.4.0
- Fruit gems: sugar coated fruit jellies in six colours (cherry, orange, lemon, lime, blueberry, grape). 2 sugar and 1 matching dye make 4 gems. Eating one gives a random good effect
- Dreidel: right click to spin it. It animates while spinning, then lands on nun, gimel, hay or shin and tells everyone nearby
- Shofar: made from a goat horn and leather, hold right click to blast it. The sound is a synthesized placeholder, replace sounds/shofar.ogg with a real recording any time
- Goat hide: shear an adult goat (the goat is not hurt, the hide regrows after 5 minutes). Cook hide in a furnace, smoker or campfire to get parchment
- Torah scroll: 2 parchment, 2 sticks, ink sac and string. Placeable on tables, desks and any block with a flat top, and breakable
- Ner tamid: a lamp that hangs from the ceiling and never goes out
- Banner pattern items stay as in 0.3.0

## 0.3.0
- Menorah candles are lit one at a time: right click a candle with flint and steel or a fire charge, empty hand puts it out. Light level grows with the number of lit candles
- Shabbat candlesticks in gold and silver, two candles lit one at a time, same controls
- Mezuzah in gold and silver, mounts on the side of a block on a slant. Right click with an empty hand to flip the slant
- Anything in the item tag `shalomcraft:candle_lighters` can light candles, add modded lighters with a data pack
- Banner: bolder, exactly centred Star of David. New loom patterns for the Israeli stripes (top and bottom) and Israeli side stripes
- Tallit: visible tzitzit hanging from the corners
- Menorah and candlesticks now need a block under them

## 0.2.0
- Kippah (head slot) and tallit (chest slot): dyeable in a crafting grid with any dye, stripes, trim and fringe stay undyed
- Kippah: 2 white wool in a row. Tallit: 6 white wool and 2 string

## 0.1.1
- Menorah: candles centred over the base and stem, wicks added, item icon fixed
- Star of David banner pattern (loom item) and Israeli stripes, with banner and shield textures
- Craftable Israeli flag banner (white banner and two blue dye)

## 0.1.0
- First release: challah, latkes, matzo, matzo ball soup, gefilte fish, rugelach, babka, gelt, afikomen
- Bagel line with cream cheese and lox toppers
- Decorative menorah block and a ShalomCraft creative tab
